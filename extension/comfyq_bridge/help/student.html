ComfyQ in Blender: quickstart

Turn your Blender scene into AI images, videos and 3D models on the class's ComfyQ server.

1. Start

  1. Open the bar: the ComfyQ tab in the 3D View sidebar (N), or Ctrl+Shift+Q.
  2. Press Connect and type your name. Your jobs carry this name in the class queue; only you can cancel them. Change it later with the pencil next to your name.

2. Make a picture or a video with the bar

  1. Choose a tab (Image, Video…) and a model. A ✓ marks the models the server runs right now; the others wait until the teacher loads them.
  2. Write a prompt: what you want to see (materials, light, style, mood). The expand button opens a bigger text box.
  3. Set up your scene and camera. Render Scene shows exactly what will be sent.
  4. Press Generate. Your job joins the queue: the card says where it is (“2nd in line”), then shows the progress.

3. Chain several steps with the graph

  1. Open the graph with the node button at the top of the bar (or the ComfyQ window's Graph switch).
  2. Presets adds a ready-made chain, for example Scene render → image. Its How to use note lists the steps.
  3. Edit the Prompt node (or the materials list of a Surfaces node).
  4. Run Graph runs what you selected (select a preset's frame to run only that one), or everything. Steps whose inputs did not change are reused, not run again.
  5. While it runs, Stop cancels the whole chain. A Cycles render shows its samples (Cycles 40/64) and keeps Blender usable; its ✕ stops it.

4. Results

  • Finished results come back into your file by themselves (pictures in the Image Editor, videos and sounds in the “ComfyQ Results” scene, 3D models beside your scene).
  • On a job card: Show (in the viewer), Open (in your computer's player), Save As…, Import.
  • A job that failed or was cancelled has Retry: the same settings and files, sent again.
  • Results Folder opens the folder of everything downloaded.

5. When something is greyed out

Hover over a greyed-out button: its tooltip says why. The usual reasons:

“Connect to ComfyQ first”Press Connect. If it fails, check the network with the teacher.
“… is not served right now”The server runs other models now. Ask the teacher, or pick a model with a ✓.
“The model's settings are still loading”Wait a second.
Retry: “… is no longer on disk”Clean Up deleted the rendered input: press Generate instead.
A warning about the SequencerPress Clean Up…: AI results in your scene's Sequencer would replace the camera in renders.
